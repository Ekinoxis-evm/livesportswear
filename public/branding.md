# LIVE! Activewear — Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Overview

LIVE! Activewear is a premium athletic-lifestyle and wellness apparel brand. The identity blends high-performance sportswear with editorial fashion sensibility. Positioning is aspirational, movement-driven, and sustainability-conscious.

- **Tagline:** Shape Your Mind
- **Supporting line:** Innovation with purpose
- **Values:** Performance, comfort, balance, lightness, giving back (every purchase funds reforestation/tree planting).
- **Collaborations:** Athlete/influencer-led edits (styled by Camila Coelho, curated by Gabriel Medina).

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone

Clean, confident, motion-oriented. Copy is short, punchy, benefit-led. Mixes wellness language ("comfort, balance, and lightness") with movement and style ("Real movement. Real style."). Eyebrow labels are uppercase and editorial. Elevated, calm, purposeful — no hype/slang.

## 3. Logo

Wordmark only: "LIVE!" — bold, uppercase, exclamation point integral to the mark. No icon/symbol. Light cream (#F4F0F0) on dark charcoal header. Use generous clear space.

## 5. Typography

Three-tier system:

- **Display (editorial headlines):** Perfectly Nineties — serif, 30–48px, weight 600 (e.g. "Buy More, Save More", "New Arrivals").
- **Headings / UI / labels:** Neue Plak (Neue Plak Extended SemiBold for nav). Uppercase, wide letter-spacing.
- **Body:** Inter — 16px, weight 400, letter-spacing 0.2px.

Specs:

- Display H1/H2: Perfectly Nineties, 36–48px, weight 600
- H2 label: Neue Plak, 20px, weight 600, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing 0.4px
- H3: Neue Plak, 16px, weight 700
- Buttons: Neue Plak, 14px, weight 600, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing 1.2px
- Nav links: Neue Plak Extended SemiBold, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing 1.28px
- Body: Inter, 16px, weight 400, letter-spacing 0.2px

Licensing: Perfectly Nineties and Neue Plak are commercial typefaces (license required for production). Free fallbacks: Fraunces or Playfair Display (display); Archivo or Space Grotesk (headings). Inter is open-source.
